# Disputing a Transaction

If you don't recognize a transaction on your account, or believe you were
charged incorrectly, you can file a dispute.

## How to File a Dispute
1. Contact Marigold Bank support within 60 days of the statement date on
   which the disputed transaction appeared.
2. Provide the transaction date, amount, and merchant/description as shown
   on your statement.
3. Describe why you're disputing it (unauthorized charge, wrong amount,
   goods/services not received, duplicate charge, etc.).

## What Happens Next
- Marigold Bank will provide a provisional credit for the disputed amount
  within 10 business days while the investigation is ongoing, for most
  cases.
- Investigations are typically resolved within 45 days, or up to 90 days for
  disputes involving transactions made outside the United States.
- If the dispute is resolved in your favor, the provisional credit becomes
  permanent. If not, the provisional credit is reversed and you will be
  notified in writing with the reason.

## Unauthorized Transactions
If your card or account credentials were lost, stolen, or compromised,
report it immediately. Your liability for unauthorized transactions is $0 if
reported within 2 business days of discovering the loss.
