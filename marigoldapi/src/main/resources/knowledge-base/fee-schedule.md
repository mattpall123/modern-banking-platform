# Marigold Bank Fee Schedule

## Checking Accounts
- Monthly maintenance fee: $0 (no minimum balance required)
- Out-of-network ATM withdrawal: $2.50 per transaction
- Overdraft fee: $30 per item, maximum of 3 per day
- Insufficient funds returned item fee: $30

## Savings Accounts
- Monthly maintenance fee: $0
- Excess withdrawal fee: $5 per withdrawal beyond 6 per statement cycle

## Transfers
- Internal transfers between Marigold Bank accounts: free, no limit
- Outgoing wire transfer: $25 per transfer
- Incoming wire transfer: free

## Other
- Paper statement fee: $3/month (waived if you opt into e-statements)
- Account closure within 90 days of opening: $25
