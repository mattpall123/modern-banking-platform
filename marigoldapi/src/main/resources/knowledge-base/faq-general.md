# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I open a new account?
Log in to your Marigold Bank account and choose "Open a new account" from
your dashboard. You can open a Checking or Savings account instantly; no
minimum opening deposit is required.

## How long do transfers take?
Transfers between your own Marigold Bank accounts complete instantly.
Transfers to accounts at other banks are not currently supported.

## Can I have more than one checking account?
Yes, there's no limit on the number of Checking or Savings accounts a
customer can hold.

## How do I close an account?
Contact support to close an account. An account closed within 90 days of
opening incurs a $25 early closure fee (see the Fee Schedule).

## Is my money insured?
Marigold Bank is a demonstration/educational banking platform and does not
hold real funds or provide real deposit insurance. Balances shown are
simulated for demonstration purposes only.

## How do I report suspicious activity?
Contact support immediately if you notice a transaction you don't
recognize. See the Dispute Process document for details on how disputes are
handled.
