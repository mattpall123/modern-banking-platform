# Overdraft Policy

Marigold Bank does not enroll customers in overdraft coverage by default. If a
transaction would take a checking account below $0, it is declined at no
charge unless the customer has opted in to overdraft coverage.

## Opted-In Overdraft Coverage
- Marigold Bank will cover transactions that overdraw the account by up to
  $500.
- Each overdraft item incurs a $30 fee, capped at 3 fees ($90) per calendar
  day.
- The account must be brought back to a positive balance within 21 calendar
  days, or the account may be restricted from further debit transactions
  until the balance is resolved.

## Savings Accounts
Savings accounts are never enrolled in overdraft coverage. A transaction that
would overdraw a savings account is declined at no charge.

## Repeated Overdrafts
An account with more than 6 overdraft occurrences in a rolling 12-month
period is flagged for manual review by an account analyst, who may reach out
to discuss account options.
